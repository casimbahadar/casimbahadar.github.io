Problems I solve

Services and process

  • Repetitive work done by hand

    Scripts and automations for the copying, sorting, renaming and report-building someone does every week.

  • A process stuck in email or spreadsheets

    Custom web apps: forms, trackers, booking and inventory tools, dashboards and customer portals.

  • Tools that don't talk to each other

    Connections between your forms, spreadsheets, calendars and databases, so nobody retypes the same information.

  • Data you can't get answers from

    Cleanup of messy records and exports, plus reports that answer the questions you actually ask.

  • No website, or an outdated one

    A fast site with the pages you need and a contact form, hosted under your own account.

  • Training people or getting them engaged

    Interactive courses with saved progress, and browser games or demos people can open from a link.

Something else? Describe it anyway. The first conversation is for working out whether software is the right fix.
