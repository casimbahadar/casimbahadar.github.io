Business demos

Small working versions of jobs businesses hire me for. Each opens with made-up sample data. Nothing you load leaves your device, except in the AI demo's live mode, which says exactly what it sends.

Email to job ticket

An AI model reads a customer email for a made-up plumbing company and drafts job tickets and a reply. Then code checks that every field quotes the email, and shows anything the model made up that the checks caught.

  • Urgency matched the expected answer for 18 of 18 jobs in its eval run
  • Every field shown is checked against the email's own words
  • Recorded samples work for anyone, and a free live mode reads your own made-up email

Customer list cleanup

Load a customer spreadsheet and get it back with spacing, capitalization, phone numbers, postal codes and dates fixed. Possible duplicates are listed for a person to confirm, and every change is logged cell by cell.

  • Tested to find exactly 42 planted fixes and 17 planted problems in its sample, with no false flags
  • Reads Shopify-style exports, French headers and files saved by Excel on Windows
  • Nothing is merged without the person choosing to
Open the demoView code

Invoice and bank reconciliation

Load invoices and a bank statement. Payments are matched by invoice number, or by amount and date, and whatever is left becomes a short list for a person, each item with the reason it was flagged.

  • Flags unpaid, partly paid and duplicate payments, and HST that isn't 13%
  • Handles RBC-style and TD-style bank export layouts, including files with no header row
  • Leftover payments can be matched by hand, and each hand match can be undone

Job request intake and tracker

A request form for a made-up plumbing company. One entry produces a priority, a job checklist, a message for the customer, a calendar file and a spreadsheet row, and the office tracks each job on a status board.

  • Priority comes from rules printed on the page
  • A gas smell shows safety steps instead of booking a visit
  • Stays in the browser and doesn't connect to any outside service

Workflow time audit

List the steps of a task you repeat and see which ones take the most hours a year, worked out only from your own numbers with the formula on screen.

  • Shows the time a task takes now, not promised savings
  • Every step shows its own arithmetic
  • Ends with a summary to copy and a CSV download

Job quote builder

Build a quote for a made-up plumbing company from a price list. The discount, 13% HST, deposit and balance are worked out as you type, with the arithmetic shown, and the quote prints or saves as a PDF.

  • Every sample quote is checked to the cent against totals worked out by hand
  • Prints on one page, with none of the screen around it
  • An accepted quote becomes a job row for the job intake demo

Overdue invoice follow-up

Load an invoice list and see what's owed, grouped by how late it is, with one reminder per customer that gets firmer as the bill gets older.

  • Tested against 25 invoices, with every total worked out by hand first

  • Reads English and French spreadsheets, including the reconciliation demo's invoice file
